# Barn finds, Discover Japan stamps, collectibles and interactive maps in Forza Horizon 6

Scope: FH6 only (launched May 2026, Japan). All sources are public web guides, May-June 2026. No official Playground or forza.net page with the stamp thresholds or barn find list turned up; everything below comes from third-party guides unless marked otherwise. Nothing here was taken from FH5; where FH5 assumptions might be tempting (e.g. "aftermarket cars"), they are listed as gaps.

## How do barn finds unlock, and what are the regional "stamps"?

### Takeaway
Barn finds are gated by the **Discover Japan** section of the **Collection Journal**. You earn Discover Japan points from exploration and regional activities, and they fill a 7-stamp ladder (Yellow to Gold). Each new stamp reveals a new batch of barn rumours (purple search zones) on the map. All 15 barn finds need the Gold stamp (Master Explorer, 20,000 points). Driving to a barn before its stamp does nothing.

### Cited Findings
- **Official design intent (developer quote).** The Collection Journal is a structured take on Japan's stamp-collecting culture. Points of interest you discover go into the Journal and drive Horizon Festival rank and overall progression. Torben Ellert, Playground design director, called it "a visual representation of your journey". This comes from an Xbox Wire interview, 22 Jan 2026. — [GamingBolt, 23 Jan 2026](https://gamingbolt.com/?p=635342); [Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/01/22/forza-horizon-6-developer-direct-breakdown-interview/)
- **Where to see progress in game:** Campaign Menu > Collection Journal > Discover Japan. Each new rating marks more barns on the map. Visiting a barn before its requirement is met does nothing. Found cars take time to repair, or you can pay to repair them instantly under Cars > Barn Finds. — [PowerPyx, 17 May 2026](https://www.powerpyx.com/forza-horizon-6-all-barn-find-locations/)
- **Seven stamps (cumulative Discover Japan points):**

  | Stamp | Title | Points | Other unlocks reported |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Yellow | Visitor | 1,250 | first barn rumour; can buy The Estate |
  | Green | Sightseer (or "Tourist") | 2,500 | barn leads in Ito |
  | Blue | Traveller | 5,000 | Hakusan Mountain Lodge |
  | Pink | Pathfinder (one source: "Explorer") | 8,000 | largest batch of barns (4) |
  | Orange | Navigator | 12,000 | Fuji Unkai House |
  | Purple | Adventurer | 16,000 | Soko 78 property |
  | Gold | Master Explorer | 20,000 | Vision House; last 2 barns; "Master Explorer" achievement |

  Sources: — [NeonLightsMedia, 21 May 2026](https://www.neonlightsmedia.com/blog/forza-horizon-6-fast-stamp-unlock-guide); point thresholds per barn also given by [Guides4Gamers](https://guides4gamers.com/forza-horizon-6/pois/barn-finds/); tier names by [AllThings.How, upd. 28 May 2026](https://allthings.how/forza-horizon-6-barn-finds-how-to-unlock-all-15-cars/)
- **Naming conflicts:** AllThings.How calls tier 2 "Tourist". NeonLightsMedia and the fan site FH6Guide call it "Sightseer". The 8,000-point tier is "Pathfinder" in most sources and "Explorer" in one ([gamingpromax](https://gamingpromax.com/forza-horizon-6-discover-japan-stamps-guide/), as summarised in search results). The thresholds are the same across sources. — [AllThings.How](https://allthings.how/forza-horizon-6-barn-finds-how-to-unlock-all-15-cars/); [NeonLightsMedia](https://www.neonlightsmedia.com/blog/forza-horizon-6-fast-stamp-unlock-guide); [FH6Guide](https://jiswarrior.github.io/Forza-Horizon-6/barn-finds.html)
- **Activities that feed Discover Japan points:**
  - story events (yellow map icons), which give the largest single chunks;
  - touge and street races;
  - road races;
  - destroying regional mascots;
  - raising food delivery rank;
  - buying houses;
  - revealing new map regions.

  — [AllThings.How](https://allthings.how/forza-horizon-6-barn-finds-how-to-unlock-all-15-cars/)

  Also cited: photography and car collecting ([gamingpromax via search summary](https://gamingpromax.com/forza-horizon-6-discover-japan-stamps-guide/)), and small point bumps for buying cars ([NeonLightsMedia](https://www.neonlightsmedia.com/blog/forza-horizon-6-fast-stamp-unlock-guide)).
- **The only per-activity number found:** a three-star yellow story event is worth about 300 points, and the game provides a loaner car for it. Mascots give "instant" payouts and touge/night street races give "strong" ones, with no numbers. — [NeonLightsMedia](https://www.neonlightsmedia.com/blog/forza-horizon-6-fast-stamp-unlock-guide)
- **Fastest route (guide advice):**
  1. Do yellow story events to 3 stars first.
  2. Smash mascots while driving between events.
  3. Chain activities inside one region (Drift Club, then a touge, then mascots, then a landmark photo).
  4. Join Mei's Day Trips, which route you past collectibles and photo spots.
  5. Use tuned cars for touge and night street races.

  — [NeonLightsMedia](https://www.neonlightsmedia.com/blog/forza-horizon-6-fast-stamp-unlock-guide)

  You don't need to clear every activity, because optional content exceeds the requirement. Inside a purple search zone, use drone mode (pause menu > My Horizon) to spot the dirt track to the barn. — [AllThings.How](https://allthings.how/forza-horizon-6-barn-finds-how-to-unlock-all-15-cars/)
- **A source conflict on what earns stamps:** one guide (summarised in search results) says stamps come from exploration, "not racing". Others explicitly list touge, street and road races. Treat the first claim as wrong or oversimplified. — search summary of [Gamer Guides](https://www.gamerguides.com/forza-horizon-6/checklists/barn-find-locations) / [VGC](https://videogameschronicle.com/guide/forza-horizon-6-barn-find-locations-how-to-unlock-and-find-all-15-barn-find-cars), contradicted by [AllThings.How](https://allthings.how/forza-horizon-6-barn-finds-how-to-unlock-all-15-cars/)

### Inferences
- The "stamps" the game mentions are the 7 Discover Japan ranks, not region-specific stamps. The "regional" feel comes from the activities: mascots, touge and story events are spread across the regions. No source describes a separate per-region stamp count.
- The Gold-stamp houses (Vision House, etc.) suggest that the same ladder gates houses as well as barns. "Buying houses" also earns points, so this forms a loop.

### Gaps
- No official (forza.net or in-game text) confirmation of the thresholds; all come from guides.
- Point values per activity (mascot, touge, food delivery, house, region reveal) were not found.
- I found no source describing per-region stamp progress, if it exists.

There are **15 barn finds** (stamp-gated) and **9 treasure cars** (one per region, collectible at any time from postcard clues), so 24 hidden cars in total. There are also **200 mascots** and **200 bonus boards**. There are 9 regions: Hokubu, Ito, Minamino, Nangan, Ohtani, Shimanoyama, Sotoyama, Takashiro and Tokyo City.

- Shackmaps' 401 against PowerPyx's 400 smashables could be one extra marker, such as the 5,000 XP waterfall board, or a counting difference. This is unverified.

### Gaps

- The barn region conflicts above (Diablo, R390, NSX, 2000GT) are unresolved without a primary map check.

## Existing interactive maps: who, how, what, licence

### Takeaway
The main free interactive maps are **Shackmaps** (Shacknews), **Gamer Guides**, **Guides4Gamers** and **Wand** (formerly WeMod, a trainer/cheat app). PowerPyx offers a static image. None of them publishes an open licence or embedding terms. Shacknews and Guides4Gamers reserve all rights. I found no FH6 map on MapGenie (the site blocks automated fetches) and could not reach a Fandom wiki page.

### Cited Findings
- **Shackmaps** — [shackmaps.com/forza-horizon-6/japan](https://shackmaps.com/forza-horizon-6/japan); announced by [Shacknews, Sam Chandler, 14 May 2026](https://www.shacknews.com/article/149151/forza-horizon-6-interactive-map-shackmaps)
  - What it marks: barn finds, treasure cars, PR stunts, bonus boards, mascots and races (counts in the previous section).
  - Free to view; a free account lets you suggest pins and add custom ones.
  - Rights: no embed code (the article links out via an image). Shacknews "reserves all rights" and prohibits use of its content for AI training or text generation.
  - Not found: the base-map source and update cadence.
- **Gamer Guides** — [gamerguides.com/forza-horizon-6](https://www.gamerguides.com/forza-horizon-6/checklists/barn-find-locations). Has an interactive map with 15 barn pins and a checklist that tracks what you've collected; also has a treasure car checklist. — search summary of [Gamer Guides](https://www.gamerguides.com/forza-horizon-6/checklists/barn-find-locations). The page returned HTTP 403 to fetch, so I could not read its terms, ad model or imagery source.
- **Guides4Gamers** — [guides4gamers.com/forza-horizon-6/pois/barn-finds/](https://guides4gamers.com/forza-horizon-6/pois/barn-finds/)
  - POI categories: Barn Finds (15), Bonus Boards, Mascots, Player Houses, Treasure Cars.
  - Lists the stamp and points for each barn.
  - Ad-supported (shows "Advertisement" slots).
  - Rights: "© 2026 Guides4Gamers. All rights reserved"; has a Terms page.
  - Imagery source: not stated.
- **Wand (WeMod)** — [wand.com/en/maps/forza-horizon-6](https://wand.com/es/maps/forza-horizon-6)
  - Categories: barn finds, bonus boards, mascots, player houses, special vehicles.
  - Images are served from api-cdn.wemod.com.
  - Promotes its desktop app for live location tracking, teleporting and nearby-pin alerts, i.e. it reads game memory or acts as a trainer.
  - Has a Terms page at /terms; its contents were not read.
- **PowerPyx** — offers a free static collectibles map image (JPG), not an interactive map. — [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/forza-horizon-6-all-collectible-locations-treasure-map-mascots-bonus-boards/)
- **FH6Guide** — a fan site on GitHub Pages (jiswarrior.github.io) with text-only directions, no coordinates and no map. "© 2026 FH6Guide", "All guide content is original"; no licence and no repository linked. — [FH6Guide](https://jiswarrior.github.io/Forza-Horizon-6/barn-finds.html)
- **Official map reveal:** forza.net published a full map reveal in April 2026. This is a possible primary base-map image; its usage terms were not checked. — [forza.net](https://forza.net/news/forza-horizon-6-full-map-reveal)

### Inferences
- The interactive maps very likely use the in-game map or the official reveal art as their base layer, since it is common practice. No site states this, so treat it as unverified.
- Wand's live tracking and teleport features conflict with the project rule against accessing game internals. Do not integrate it or link to it as a data source.
- No site offers reuse or embedding rights. An fh-hub feature should link out, not embed or scrape.

### Gaps
- Terms text for Shackmaps, Gamer Guides, Wand and MapGenie was not read (pages truncated, 403, or not fetched).
- Update cadence was not stated by any map.
- No Reddit or YouTube sources surfaced in search ("all barn finds" videos, creators, dates). PC Gamer reportedly has a video version of its guide (search summary only).
- The Fandom wiki ([forza.fandom.com](https://forza.fandom.com)) returned HTTP 402 to fetch. Fandom text is normally CC BY-SA, but I could not confirm that an FH6 barn find page exists.

## Public data sets with coordinates

### Takeaway
I found no public spreadsheet or wiki table with in-game coordinates for FH6 barn finds or collectibles. The available data is text directions, screenshots and pins inside proprietary interactive maps.

### Cited Findings
- FH6Guide gives text directions only, with no coordinates. — [FH6Guide](https://jiswarrior.github.io/Forza-Horizon-6/barn-finds.html)
- Guides4Gamers links each barn to a "Show the map" anchor; its pin coordinates are not published as data. — [Guides4Gamers](https://guides4gamers.com/forza-horizon-6/pois/barn-finds/)

### Inferences
- The pin data inside Shackmaps or Gamer Guides may be fetchable via their front-end APIs. That would be scraping third-party IP without permission, and the visible terms reserve rights, so it is not advisable.

### Gaps
- I did not search for Google Sheets, GitHub data repos or Reddit community spreadsheets specifically; a targeted search could still find one.
